What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email, phone number, company and password (stored securely).
  • Demo and contact requests: what you enter in our forms, such as your use case and notes.
  • Your contacts: lists of people you upload so Remo can call them.
  • Call data: call times, outcomes, transcripts and, where enabled, recordings.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To run Remo, place calls and show you your results.
  • To set up demos and reply to your requests.
  • To send account emails such as confirmations and password resets.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • To improve how Remo sounds and performs.

Calls & recordings

Remo speaks with the people on your contact list on your behalf. You decide who is called and are responsible for having the right to contact them and for any consent needed to record. We may tell people on a call that they are speaking with an AI assistant.

Call recordings can be played and downloaded only by the account that made the call and by the TakeRemo owner, after signing in. There are no public or shareable links. Every recording is permanently deleted 30 days after the call ends; if you need one for longer, download it within that time. The transcript, summary and screening result are kept.

Who we share with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers that help us run TakeRemo, such as phone, voice and AI, email and hosting providers, and only as needed to provide the service. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal duties, except call recordings, which are deleted 30 days after the call ends. You can ask us to delete your data, and we will do so unless we must keep it by law.

Security

We use sensible safeguards to protect your information, including access controls and keeping each account's data separate. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Your choices

  • Ask to see the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Opt out of non-essential emails at any time.

If someone on a call asks not to be contacted again, tell us and we will help you respect that.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and we will highlight important updates.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Reach us through our contact page. You can also read our Terms of Service.
