Compliance, honestly

Who is responsible for what.

Remo makes the calls you ask it to make. Keeping those calls lawful is a job we share, so here it is in plain words.

TakeRemo

  • Runs the service securely and keeps each account's data separate.
  • Never sells your information.
  • Shares data only with the providers needed to run the service: phone, voice and AI, email and hosting.
  • Lets you see, correct or delete your data, unless the law says we must keep it.
  • May tell people on a call that they are speaking with an AI assistant.

You

  • Choose who is called, and make sure you have the right to contact them.
  • Get any consent needed before calls are recorded.
  • Tell us when someone asks not to be contacted again, so we can help you respect it.
  • Use a strong, unique password for your account.
  • Check the rules for your region and industry before you start calling.
